OVERGROWTH ONLINE (friends test build)
======================================

Online co-op for the Overgrowth and Lugaru stories, arena PvP, and the
Overgrowth Online mod (smarter AI, HUD, weather, trainer: F9 in any level),
through Steam. Everyone needs Overgrowth on Steam and this same
package.

INSTALL
  1. Close Overgrowth.
  2. Run install.bat. It finds Overgrowth through Steam. If it can't, run:
       install.bat "D:\SteamLibrary\steamapps\common\Overgrowth"
  3. Start Overgrowth from Steam as usual. Steam must be running.

  - Hold Shift while Overgrowth starts to get the normal game.
  - Overgrowth Online keeps its own settings and mods in
    Documents\Wolfire\OvergrowthOnline. Your normal game is not changed.
  - Had Overhaul Online (the old name)? Installing replaces it and keeps
    your settings.

YOUR CHARACTER
  Main menu > Multiplayer > Your character:
  - Name: 3 or more Latin letters or digits. Empty = your Steam name.
  - Character: any rabbit, cat, dog, rat or wolf. Or the level's own
    character (the host plays the story's hero).
  - Fur and clothes colours.
  Everyone sees your choice. It applies in every level.

PLAY TOGETHER
  Host:
    Main menu > Multiplayer, then pick Overgrowth Story or Lugaru Story and a
    level. In the level: Esc > Invite friends (Steam's overlay opens).
  Joining:
    Main menu > Multiplayer > Join a friend: your Steam friends who host are
    listed (what they play, how many players). Press Join. Friends can join a
    host as soon as the host's level has loaded, no invite needed.
    Or accept an invite in Steam (chat or overlay), or Steam friends list >
    right-click them > Join Game. If Overgrowth isn't running, Steam starts it
    and joins.
  Leaving:
    Esc > Disconnect (a guest), or Main Menu.

  How co-op works:
    - The host runs the enemies and the story. Guests follow the host to the
      next level when the host finishes one.
    - Dialogues and cutscenes are the host's: guests watch them, and their
      characters stand still meanwhile. Only the host can skip.
    - Anyone can talk to a "?" character: the dialogue plays for everyone.
    - A player who is down gets up at the next checkpoint. When everyone is
      down, the level starts over.
    - Or revive them: crouch and hold grab (right click) on their body for
      3 seconds. A bar shows how far along it is.
    - Trainer cheats (F9 > Trainer) are the host's. Players get their own
      only if the host ticks "Let players use the Trainer".
    - Weather and time of day (F9 > Weather) are the host's too: everyone
      gets the host's sky, rain or snow.

ARENA (PvP)
  Main menu > Multiplayer > Arena (PvP), then a map: Waterfall Cave, Magma
  Arena, Rock Cave or Stucco Courtyard. Invite friends as above.
    - Players only, no AI. Everyone fights for themselves.
    - Each match uses one of the map's arena setups: the spots, and in weapon
      matches everyone's weapon. A weapon match is a fight to the death (one
      point). The other matches are two points to win.
    - The last one standing wins the round. Points are at the top left.
    - Someone who joins during a round fights from the next round.

UNINSTALL
  Close Overgrowth and run uninstall.bat. The normal Overgrowth.exe comes back.
  Steam's "Verify integrity of game files" also removes the launcher. Run
  install.bat again to get it back.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  Send Documents\Wolfire\OvergrowthOnline\logfile.txt to whoever sent you this.
